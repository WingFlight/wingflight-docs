    Dashboard Themes¶

    The dashboard widget comes with several themes. A theme decides what the dashboard shows and how it looks. Each theme has three layouts: preflight before you arm, inflight while you fly, and postflight after you land. The dashboard switches between them by itself.

    Choose the theme under Settings → Dashboard: one theme for all three phases or a different one for each, and optionally a different theme for a particular model. Themes with their own settings have a tile under Settings → Dashboard → Settings.

    Theme Own settings Screen
    Default Yes Any
    AERC Yes Any
    AERC Nitro Yes Any
    Basic Timer Any
    Claude Yes Any
    DanielRC Any
    Gismo Yes Any

    SRB-RC Yes Any

    Default¶

    A large voltage arc beside the flight time and link quality. Before the flight it also shows the model image, profiles, flight count and Blackbox use.

    AERC¶

    Arcs for throttle, RPM and ESC temperature, each with its maximum, under the flight time and a fuel bar with pack voltage and mAh used.

    AERC Nitro¶

    For nitro models: RPM and throttle arcs either side of a segmented BEC voltage bar, with the flight time on top.

    Basic Timer¶

    A large flight timer, with the profiles, flight count and link quality below it.

    Claude¶

    A voltage arc, a large fuel ring and an RPM dial, with the timer and governor state.

    DanielRC¶

    A text panel in three groups (power, RPM and status, or the second motor on twins) with the governor state and flight time.

    Gismo¶

    A large battery graphic between RPM and timer on one side, and cell and pack voltage on the other.

    Kevd¶

    Arcs for ESC temperature, RPM and throttle with their maximums, beside a column with the timer, fuel and governor state.

    Needs a dashboard area of at least 784 × 294 pixels, so it is hidden on smaller radios such as the X18.

    KSE4¶

    A tile dashboard after Kyle Stacy's KSE4: a large RPM tile beside the timer, a strip of current, voltage, ESC temperature and throttle, and a full-width fuel bar. It takes its colours from the radio's Ethos theme.

    KSE5¶

    A ring dashboard after Kyle Stacy's KSE5: rings for fuel, RPM, current and ESC temperature, with the governor, voltages and mAh used in tiles below. Its settings set the full scale of each ring. It takes its colours from the radio's Ethos theme.

    RF Status¶

    Voltage and fuel arcs above the governor state, link quality, RPM and timer.

    RT-RC¶

    Pack voltage and fuel arcs above the governor state, link quality, RPM and timer. Before the flight it adds the model image, profiles, flight count and Blackbox use.

    RT-RC Nitro¶

    RT-RC for nitro models: BEC voltage and throttle arcs in place of pack voltage and fuel.

    SRB-RC¶

    A grid of tiles: governor state, BEC voltage, link, RPM, profiles, ESC temperature, current and timer above a full-width flight battery bar.

    The screenshots come from the Ethos simulator, so the values are examples. The postflight screens use sample flight figures.
